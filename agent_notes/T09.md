# T09: Seeded PRNG & Deterministic Map Generator (`src/core/mapGen.ts`)

Exports:
- `mulberry32(seed: number): () => number` and `createMulberry32(seed: number): SeededRng` (`next`, `nextInt`, `nextFloat`, `pick`). Fails closed (`SimulationError`) on non-finite seeds or invalid ranges.
- `generateMap(seed: number, options?: MapGenOptions): GeneratedMap` — generates a row-major `Tile[]` (default `64×64`, `spawnCenter = (32, 32)`). Guarantees non-water starting patches of `iron_ore`, `copper_ore`, `coal`, and `stone` in 4 quadrants near `spawnCenter` (within ~15 tiles) and returns `startingPatches: Record<ResourceType, readonly TileCoord[]>`.
- `createInitialWorldState(seed: number, options?: MapGenOptions): WorldState` — convenience builder returning a fresh `schemaVersion: 1` `WorldState` at `tick: 0`.
