# T28: Steam Power (`src/machines/steamPower.ts`)

Exports `STEAM_ENGINE_MAX_OUTPUT_KW` (`900`), `BOILER_MAX_STEAM_BUFFER` (`100`), `KJ_PER_STEAM_UNIT` (`15`), `BOILER_MAX_STEAM_PER_TICK` (`2`), `BOILER_MAX_POWER_KW` (`1800`), `isBoiler`, `isSteamEngine`, `areTilesAdjacent`, `getAdjacentBoilers`, `insertBoilerFuel`, `updateBoiler`, `updateSteamEngine`, and `updateSteamPower`.

- **Energy & Fuel Conservation:** 1 unit of steam equals `15 kJ` (`900 kW / 60 ticks/s`). A `boiler` burns coal (`4000 kJ` from `itemRegistry`) up to `2` steam/tick (`1800 kW` thermal, enough for 2 adjacent 900 kW steam engines) only while `steamBuffer < 100`. When `steamBuffer` is full, the boiler halts without wasting fuel.
- **Boiler <-> Steam Engine Coupling:** A `steam_engine` draws steam from orthogonally adjacent (`|dx| + |dy| === 1`) boilers in ascending `EntityId` order, setting `currentOutputKw` proportionally up to `maxPowerOutputKw` (`900 kW` default) and `active = currentOutputKw > 0`.
- **Fail-Closed & Determinism:** `insertBoilerFuel` rejects non-fuel items with `SimulationError('INVALID_FUEL_ITEM')`. Negative or non-finite `fuelEnergyKj`, `steamBuffer`, or `maxPowerOutputKw` throw `SimulationError('INVALID_STEAM_POWER_STATE')`. All entity iterations sort by ascending `EntityId`.
- **Downstream Notes:** Call `updateSteamPower(world)` during the `power` tick phase before `solvePowerGrid(world)` so `steam_engine.currentOutputKw` is populated for `T18`.
