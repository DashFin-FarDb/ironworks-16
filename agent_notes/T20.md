# T20: Research & Tech Tree (`src/logistics/techTree.ts`)

- Exports `TechTree`, `createTechTree`, `createInitialResearchState`, `CANONICAL_TECH_DEFINITIONS`, `TECH_IDS`, `TechStatus`, `ContributeScienceOptions`, and `ContributeResult`.
- Canonical DAG:
  - `automation` (`[]`, `10 red_science`) -> unlocks `electronic_circuit`, `inserter`
  - `logistics` (`['automation']`, `20 red_science`) -> unlocks `green_science`
  - `steel_processing` (`['automation']`, `25 red_science`) -> unlocks `steel_plate`
  - `solar_energy` (`['logistics', 'steel_processing']`, `30 red_science + 30 green_science`) -> unlocks `power_pole`
- Item conservation: `contributeScience(state, itemId, count, { tick, eventBus })` returns `{ techId, itemId, accepted, rejected, completed }` so `ResearchLab` (T30) only consumes `accepted` packs and never loses excess or mismatched packs.
- EventBus integration: emits `researchCompleted` (`{ tick, techId }`) when `eventBus` is provided and all required science packs are satisfied.
- Recipe gating: `getRequiredTechForRecipe`, `isRecipeUnlocked`, `assertRecipeUnlocked`, `getLockedRecipes`, and `getUnlockedRecipes` gate locked recipes until their tech is in `state.completedTechs`.
- Fail-closed: throws `SimulationError` on unknown tech IDs, unmet prerequisites, already-completed techs, circular dependencies, invalid science counts, or locked recipes (`assertRecipeUnlocked`).
