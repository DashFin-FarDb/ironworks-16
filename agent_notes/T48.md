# Agent Note: T48 - Showcase Factory

## Overview
Implemented `src/app/showcaseFactory.ts` and unit test `tests/T48_showcaseFactory.test.ts`.

## Architecture & Blueprint
- Deterministic blueprint builder `buildShowcaseFactory` targeting seed `1616`.
- Connects coal-fed Boiler + Steam Engine supplying 900 kW electrical power.
- Distributes power through interconnected Power Poles to all mining, smelting, and assembling machinery.
- Operates electric mining drills on guaranteed iron & copper patches.
- Connects smelting furnaces, gear assemblers, and an Automation Science Pack assembler (`red_science`).
- Encapsulates deterministic execution into `createShowcaseHarness(seed)`, providing `step(ticks)` and `getTelemetry()` exposing active entities, power satisfaction (> 0.9), total items on belts, and red science produced.
