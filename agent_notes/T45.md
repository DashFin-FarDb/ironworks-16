# T45: Live Production Sparkline Graph (`src/ui/productionGraph.ts`)

Implemented `ProductionGraph`, `createProductionGraph()`, `computeSparklinePoints()`, `formatRatePerMinute()`, and `renderProductionGraph()` for live items/min sparklines of `iron_plate`, `copper_plate`, `iron_gear`, and `red_science`.

## Exports
- `PRODUCTION_GRAPH_ITEMS`: `['iron_plate', 'copper_plate', 'iron_gear', 'red_science']`
- `DEFAULT_SERIES_STYLES`, `DEFAULT_MAX_SAMPLES` (`60`), `DEFAULT_SAMPLE_INTERVAL_TICKS` (`60`), `DEFAULT_GRAPH_RECT`
- `ProductionGraph`, `createProductionGraph(options?)`
- `computeSparklinePoints(samples, bounds, minScale?)`
- `formatRatePerMinute(rate)`
- `renderProductionGraph(ctx, graph, rect?)`
- Structural `ProductionGraphCanvasContext` interface (compatible with `CanvasRenderingContext2D` and headless mock contexts)

## Key Design & Edge Cases
- **Tick-Driven Determinism:** Sampling is driven exclusively by simulation `tick` (`sample(stats, tick, force?)` / `sampleFromWorld(world, force?)`) with `sampleIntervalTicks` throttling (default 60 ticks = 1 sample/sec) and bounded FIFO history (`maxSamples = 60`). No `Date.now()` or `Math.random()`.
- **Fail-Closed & Atomic:** Validates options, `ItemId`s (via `assertValidItemId`), monotonic non-negative integer ticks, finite non-negative rates, and positive bounds/rects. Invalid `sample()` inputs throw `SimulationError` before mutating any series.
- **Zero-NaN Sparkline Scaling:** `computeSparklinePoints` auto-scales Y with `max(minScale, peak)` (`minScale = 1`) so all-zero and single-point series render cleanly at the baseline with zero `NaN`.
- **Integration for T50 (`main.ts`):** Call `graph.sampleFromWorld(world)` each tick (or after `statsTracker.syncToWorld(world)`) and `renderProductionGraph(ctx, graph, rect)` in the HUD render pass.
