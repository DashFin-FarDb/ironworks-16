# T44: Clicked Entity Inspector (`src/ui/inspector.ts`)

Implemented the deterministic clicked-entity inspector view-model, selection state helpers, and Canvas 2D / HTML renderers covering all 19 `EntityType` variants in `src/types/contracts.ts`.

## Exports
- **State Helpers:** `createInspectorState`, `selectEntity`, `selectEntityAtTile`, `clearInspectorSelection` (`InspectorState`).
- **Inspection API:** `inspectEntity(world, entityId, recipeRegistry?)`, `inspectSelectedEntity(state, world, recipeRegistry?)` returning `InspectorSnapshot | null` (includes `inventories`, `progress`, `fuel`, `power`, `statusText`, `recipeId`, and `details`).
- **Formatters & Renderers:** `formatInspectorLines`, `renderInspectorHTML`, `renderInspectorCanvas`.
- **Constants:** `ENTITY_DISPLAY_NAMES`, `BURNER_DRILL_MAX_MINING_TICKS` (90), `ELECTRIC_DRILL_MAX_MINING_TICKS` (45), `RESEARCH_LAB_MAX_TICKS` (60), `BOILER_MAX_STEAM_BUFFER` (100), `DEFAULT_FUEL_MAX_KJ` (4000).

## Key Design Choices & Edge Cases
- **Headless-Safe & Deterministic:** Pure inspection logic has zero DOM/Canvas imports or `Math.random`/`Date.now` calls; `renderInspectorCanvas` only touches the passed `CanvasRenderingContext2D`.
- **Fail-Closed & NaN-Safe:** Out-of-bounds tile clicks, empty tiles, or destroyed entity IDs return `null`. All progress, fuel, and power ratios are guarded against `NaN`/`Infinity`/zero-max and clamped to `[0, 1]`.
- **Downstream Integration (`T47`, `T50`):** `T47` can call `selectEntityAtTile(inspectorState, world, tileCoord)` on left-click; `T50` can call `inspectSelectedEntity(inspectorState, world, recipeRegistry)` and pass the snapshot to `renderInspectorCanvas` or `renderInspectorHTML`.
