# T22: Burner Mining Drill (`src/machines/burnerDrill.ts`)

Implemented the deterministic, headless, fail-closed coal-fueled Burner Mining Drill over `BurnerDrillEntity` from `src/types/contracts.ts`.

## Exports
- Constants: `BURNER_DRILL_MINING_TICKS = 90`, `BURNER_DRILL_POWER_KW = 150`, `BURNER_DRILL_FUEL_BURN_KJ_PER_TICK = 2.5` (exact IEEE-754 at 60 TPS), `BURNER_DRILL_FUEL_SLOTS = 1`.
- Factory & helpers: `createBurnerDrill`, `getBurnerDrillOutputCoord`, `insertBurnerDrillFuel` (fail-closed on non-fuel items with `SimulationError('INVALID_FUEL_ITEM')`).
- Simulation: `tickBurnerDrill`, `tickAllBurnerDrills`.

## Key Design Choices & Edge Cases
- **Front-Tile Output & Conservation:** Uses `stepCoord(drill.position, drill.direction, 1)` for the front tile and reuses `dropIntoEntity` (`src/logistics/inserter.ts`) paired with a non-mutating `canDropIntoEntity` pre-check. Supports belts (right lane first, fallback left at offset `0.5`), chests, furnaces (auto-routing coal to `fuelInventory`), assemblers, labs, boilers, and other burner drills.
- **Zero-Waste Clean Halts:** Halts with `active = false` and `fuelBurnedKj = 0` on `'no_resource'`, `'out_of_bounds_output'`, `'output_blocked'`, or `'no_fuel'`. Out-of-fuel and blocked halts preserve `miningProgress` until resolved.
- **Depletion:** Uses `mineOneFromTile` (`T21`) atomically upon drop completion so depleted deposits are cleared (`tile.resource = null`) immediately.
- **Events & Determinism:** Emits `itemProduced` on the optional `EventBus` when an ore is mined. `tickAllBurnerDrills` iterates `burner_drill` entities in ascending `EntityId` order.
