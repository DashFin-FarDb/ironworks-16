# T30: Research Lab (`src/machines/researchLab.ts`)

- Exports `RESEARCH_LAB_POWER_DEMAND_KW` (60), `RESEARCH_LAB_INPUT_SLOTS` (2), `RESEARCH_LAB_TICKS_PER_PACK` (60), `SCIENCE_PACK_ITEMS`, `createResearchLab`, `isSciencePack`, `canInsertSciencePack`, `insertSciencePack`, `updateResearchLab`, and `updateAllResearchLabs`.
- Power scaling: reads `getEntityPowerSatisfaction(world, lab)` (with optional `powerSatisfactionOverride` for isolated tests) and scales progress via `scaleMachineSpeed(1, satisfaction)`. Unpowered or unconnected labs halt cleanly (`active = false`, zero progress).
- Item conservation: `insertSciencePack` rejects non-science items fail-closed (`INVALID_SCIENCE_PACK`); `updateResearchLab` only removes `result.accepted` packs from `lab.inputInventory` after `techTree.contributeScience(...)`, preserving excess or unneeded packs when a technology completes.
- Multi-pack techs: consumes 1 required science pack per `ticksPerPack` cycle in deterministic `TechDefinition.cost` order, supporting both single-pack (`automation`) and multi-pack (`solar_energy`) technologies.
- Determinism: `updateAllResearchLabs` iterates all `research_lab` entities in ascending numeric `EntityId` order.
