# T23: Electric Drill (`src/machines/electricDrill.ts`)

- **Exports:** `ELECTRIC_DRILL_POWER_DEMAND_KW` (`90`), `ELECTRIC_DRILL_MINING_TICKS` (`45`), `ELECTRIC_DRILL_BASE_SPEED` (`1`), `ELECTRIC_DRILL_FOOTPRINT` (`1x1`), `createElectricDrill`, `getElectricDrillOutputCoord`, `tryDeliverOreToFrontTile`, `tickElectricDrill`, `tickAllElectricDrills`, `ElectricDrillHaltReason`, `DrillOutputHandler`, `ElectricDrillTickContext`, and `ElectricDrillTickResult`.
- **Power & Speed Scaling:** Queries `getEntityPowerSatisfaction(world, drill)` and scales base speed (`1.0`) via `scaleMachineSpeed` from T18. At 100% satisfaction it mines 1 ore per 45 ticks; at 50% brownout it mines 1 ore per 90 ticks. Unconnected drills or 0% power halt cleanly with `active = false`, `haltReason = 'no_power'`, and zero state mutation.
- **Item & Mass Conservation:** Peeks the first non-depleted resource tile under the drill's footprint (row-major order) and attempts delivery to the front tile (`stepCoord(position, direction)`) before decrementing `ResourceNode.amount`. Supports `transport_belt`, `splitter`, `wooden_chest`, `iron_chest`, `stone_furnace`, `electric_furnace`, `assembler_mk1`, `assembler_mk2`, `burner_drill` (coal), and `boiler` (coal), plus an optional `tryDropItem` override in `ElectricDrillTickContext`. If blocked (full, empty ground, or out of bounds), `miningProgress` clamps at `45` with `haltReason = 'output_blocked'` and zero ore is lost; delivery resumes immediately on the next powered tick once space frees up.
- **Depletion & Events:** Uses `mineOneFromTile` from T21 so depleted deposits clear `tile.resource = null` immediately, halting subsequent ticks with `haltReason = 'no_resource'`. Emits `itemProduced` on `eventBus` when provided.
- **Determinism & Fail-Closed:** `tickAllElectricDrills` iterates `electric_drill` entities in ascending `EntityId` order. Throws `SimulationError` on negative/NaN `powerDemandKw` or `miningProgress` or invalid footprints, and `OutOfBoundsError` if the drill's footprint is out of bounds.
