# T24: Stone Furnace (`src/machines/stoneFurnace.ts`)

Implemented the deterministic, headless, fail-closed coal-fueled Stone Furnace smelter over `StoneFurnaceEntity` from `src/types/contracts.ts`.

## Exports
- Constants: `STONE_FURNACE_CRAFTING_SPEED = 1`, `STONE_FURNACE_POWER_KW = 90`, `STONE_FURNACE_FUEL_BURN_KJ_PER_TICK = 1.5` (at 60 TPS), `STONE_FURNACE_INPUT_SLOTS = 1`, `STONE_FURNACE_OUTPUT_SLOTS = 1`, `STONE_FURNACE_FUEL_SLOTS = 1`.
- Factory & inventory helpers: `createStoneFurnace`, `insertFurnaceInput`, `insertFurnaceFuel` (fail-closed on non-fuel items), `extractFurnaceOutput`.
- Simulation: `selectSmeltingRecipe`, `tickStoneFurnace`, `tickAllStoneFurnaces`.

## Key Design Choices & Edge Cases
- **Auto-Recipe Selection:** Uses `recipeRegistry.findSmeltingRecipeForInput()` on the first item in `inputInventory`, and gates against `completedTechs` via `recipeRegistry.isRecipeUnlocked()` (e.g., `steel_plate` requires `steel_processing`). Switching input resets `craftingProgress` cleanly.
- **Strict Item Conservation:** Ingredients remain in `inputInventory` until `craftingProgress >= recipe.craftingTicks`, at which point `consumeIngredients` and `insertItem` execute atomically and emit `itemProduced` on the optional `EventBus`.
- **Backpressure & Fuel Conservation:** If `no_input`, `no_recipe`, `recipe_locked`, `insufficient_ingredients`, or `output_full`, the furnace halts cleanly (`active = false`) and burns `0` kJ of fuel. Out-of-fuel halts preserve `craftingProgress` until refueled.
- **Determinism:** `tickAllStoneFurnaces` iterates `stone_furnace` entities in ascending `EntityId` order.
