# T04: Entity Manager (`src/core/entityManager.ts`)

Exports `EntityManager`, `EntityNotFoundError`, `createEntityInstance`, `getEntityFootprint`, `getFootprintTiles`, and `isValidEntityType`.
- **Lifecycle:** `spawn(type, position, options?)`, `rotate(id, stepsClockwise = 1)`, `destroy(id)`.
- **Queries:** `has(id)`, `getById(id)`, `getByIdOrThrow(id)`, `getAt(coord)`, `getByType(type)` (sorted by ascending `id`), `getAll()` (sorted by ascending `id`), `count()`, `countByType(type)`, `toRecord()`, `getNextEntityId()`.
- **Hydration:** `EntityManager.fromWorldState(world)` restores entities, occupancy, bounds, and monotonic `nextEntityId`.
- **Invariants:** Strictly monotonic IDs starting at `1` (never reused after `destroy`); atomic fail-closed validation (`OutOfBoundsError`, `InvalidPlacementError`, `EntityNotFoundError`, `SimulationError`) without mutating `nextEntityId`.
